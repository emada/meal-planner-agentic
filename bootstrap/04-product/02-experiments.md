# Discovery through experiments

Record every uncertain product decision as a hypothesis.

If the project starts with a problem rather than a specification, preserve the original input in `GOAL.md`. Conduct discovery with one lead agent. Convert human-approved outcomes into `SPEC.md` before production implementation. Record later hypotheses, evidence, and learning under `docs/product/experiments/`.

## Template

```text
Hypothesis:
For [audience], we believe [change] will produce [outcome].

Evidence sought:
[measurable behaviour or feedback]

Smallest experiment:
[minimum version that enables learning]

Decision after experiment:
[keep, adjust, discard, or investigate further]
```

## Rules

- Prefer the smallest delivery that enables learning.
- Do not treat vanity metrics as evidence of value.
- Product data must comply with privacy rules.
- Technical correctness does not prove usefulness.
