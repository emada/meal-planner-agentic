# Engineering quality is not product-market fit

## Engineering quality

This is a control problem.

```text
State → encode → verify → block deviations
```

Signals: tests, types, coverage, mutation score, security findings, cycles, duplication, flakes, and performance.

## Product-market fit

This is a discovery problem.

```text
Hypothesis → experiment → behaviour → learning
```

Signals: activation, retention, time-to-value, usage frequency, qualitative feedback, and business metrics.

## Rule

Gates make quality inevitable. Experiments discover whether a product is worth building. Do not mix the playbooks.
