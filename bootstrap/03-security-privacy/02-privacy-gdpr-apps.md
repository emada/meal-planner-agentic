# Privacy, GDPR, and APPs

Collect the minimum data. Use it only for explicit purposes. Protect people by default.

## Required questions before collecting data

1. Is the data necessary?
2. What specific purpose does it serve?
3. What legal basis applies?
4. Where is it stored and processed?
5. Who can access it?
6. What is the retention period?
7. How can it be accessed, corrected, exported, or deleted when applicable?
8. Can the feature be delivered without it?

## Guardrails

- data minimization;
- explicit purpose limitation;
- private defaults;
- planned retention and deletion;
- encryption and least-privilege access;
- remove or redact PII from logs;
- justify cookies, analytics, and trackers;
- record subprocessors and international transfers;
- perform privacy impact review for material changes.

Automation does not replace legal judgment. Escalate legal decisions to appropriate human review.
