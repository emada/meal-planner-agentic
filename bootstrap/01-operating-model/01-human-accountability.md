# Human accountability

Agents accelerate delivery. They do not own outcomes.

## Rules

- Every merged change has one accountable human owner.
- The merge approver owns the outcome regardless of who wrote the code.
- Agents must state assumptions, uncertainty, and evidence. Do not state assumptions as facts.
- Product, architecture, security, and privacy decisions require risk-proportionate human review.
- Do not use agents to hide complexity or outsource judgment.

## Required owner evidence

Before integration, the owner can explain:

- the problem addressed;
- the implemented change;
- the main risks;
- the verification performed;
- the rollback or remediation path.
